About AdSense Checker

AdSense Checker was built to resolve a major pain point for digital publishers: the ambiguity of Google AdSense site approvals.

Our Mission

Getting rejected by Google AdSense under vague headers like "Low Value Content" or "Policy Violation" can be frustrating. Our mission is to democratize SEO knowledge and webmaster compliance by offering automated diagnostic audits and clear guidance.

Client-Side Technology

We believe in user privacy and fast tools. Our audit tools run entirely client-side inside your browser environment—no remote database logging, no credentials tracking, and zero API costs.

Important Disclaimer

AdSense Checker is an independent diagnostic resource tool. We are not officially affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Google LLC or the Google AdSense program.
