What is Google AdSense & How It Works (2026 Guide)

Published by AdSenseChecker Team | Updated 2026

Google AdSense remains the industry standard for independent content monetization. Understanding how its underlying auction engine operates allows webmasters to build compliant, high-performing revenue sites.

1. The Core Architecture

AdSense connects website publishers with advertisers using the Google Ads network. When space is designated on your web page, real-time programmatic bidding occurs. Advertisers bid for impression inventory matching user interests and page topics.

2. Revenue Models: CPC vs. RPM

Publishers are typically paid via Cost-Per-Click (CPC) or cost-per-thousand impressions (RPM).

  • CPC: Earned revenue each time a visitor clicks an ad.
  • RPM: Revenue earned per 1,000 page views generated.

3. Qualifying for Enrollment

To qualify, websites must meet Google's strict publisher policies, demonstrate original non-duplicate content, incorporate standard legal pages, and serve pages securely over SSL (HTTPS). Run your domain through our Free Diagnostic Audit Tool before applying.
