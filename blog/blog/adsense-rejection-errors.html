Top 10 Google AdSense Rejection Errors & How to Fix Them (2026 Guide)

Published by AdSenseChecker Team | Updated 2026

Getting rejected by Google AdSense can be frustrating, especially after spending weeks building a website. The good news is that most rejections happen because of common issues that can be fixed. This guide explains the most frequent AdSense rejection reasons and practical ways to improve your website before applying again.

1. Low Value Content

One of the most common rejection reasons is low-value content. This usually means your website doesn't provide enough original, helpful, or detailed information for visitors.

How to Fix

  • Publish original articles.
  • Write detailed guides instead of short posts.
  • Avoid duplicate or AI-generated spam.
  • Update outdated articles regularly.

2. Insufficient Content

Websites with only a few pages often don't provide enough value for visitors.

How to Fix

  • Create 15–30 quality articles.
  • Add About, Contact, FAQ, and Privacy Policy pages.
  • Cover your niche thoroughly.

3. Missing Policy Pages

Important pages help visitors understand who operates the website and how their information is handled.

Essential Pages

  • About Us
  • Contact
  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms & Conditions
  • Disclaimer

4. Poor Website Navigation

Visitors should easily find information on your website.

  • Use a clear navigation menu.
  • Add internal links.
  • Create categories for articles.
  • Fix broken links.

5. Copyrighted Content

Publishing copyrighted text, images, videos, or downloads without permission can lead to rejection.

How to Fix

  • Use original writing.
  • Create your own graphics or use properly licensed images.
  • Always respect copyright.

6. Slow Website Speed

A slow website can provide a poor user experience.

  • Compress images.
  • Minify CSS and JavaScript.
  • Remove unnecessary scripts.
  • Optimize loading performance.

7. Poor Mobile Experience

Many visitors browse on mobile devices, so your site should be responsive.

  • Responsive layout.
  • Readable text.
  • Proper spacing.
  • Easy navigation.

8. Website Under Construction

Do not apply if your website contains unfinished pages or placeholder content.

Complete Your Website First

  • Finish all important pages.
  • Remove "Coming Soon" sections.
  • Publish complete articles.

9. Thin or Duplicate Pages

Multiple pages with nearly identical content can reduce the overall value of your site.

  • Merge similar articles.
  • Expand short pages.
  • Remove duplicate content.

10. Technical Problems

Technical issues can affect the review process.

  • Fix 404 errors.
  • Submit a sitemap.
  • Verify Search Console.
  • Enable HTTPS.
  • Check robots.txt.

Website Checklist Before Reapplying

  • ✔ High-quality original articles
  • ✔ About page
  • ✔ Contact page
  • ✔ Privacy Policy
  • ✔ Terms & Conditions
  • ✔ Disclaimer
  • ✔ FAQ
  • ✔ Mobile-friendly design
  • ✔ Fast loading speed
  • ✔ HTTPS enabled
  • ✔ Sitemap submitted
  • ✔ Search Console connected
  • ✔ Google Analytics connected

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply again after rejection?

Yes. After improving your website and addressing the issues identified during your review, you can submit another application.

How long should I wait?

There is no fixed waiting period. It's generally better to reapply only after you've made meaningful improvements to your website.

Does adding more articles guarantee approval?

No. Quality is more important than quantity. Focus on creating original, helpful content and a good user experience.

Final Thoughts

An AdSense rejection doesn't mean your website can't succeed. Use the feedback to improve your content, navigation, and technical quality. A well-maintained website that serves visitors effectively is more likely to meet Google's expectations over time.
